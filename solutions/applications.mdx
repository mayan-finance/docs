Mayan is embedded as the movement rails inside trading apps, aggregators and DeFi products. Teams including LI.FI, Jumper, Bungee and SwapKit route through it. Users see the app's own interface; Mayan handles routing and execution underneath.

Your users are spread across ecosystems, and the assets they hold are somewhere else again.

## Every ecosystem, without adding them one by one

Supporting a new chain or a new asset is not a new integration. Any token with a contract address is reachable, with no whitelist and no listing process, so your coverage grows without your engineering work growing with it.

## Meet users where their assets already are

Someone holding on Ethereum can enter your app on Solana without leaving to bridge first. The move happens inside your product, which is where you want them to stay.

## Use cases

- [Swap](/use-cases/swap) — any-to-any trading inside the app
- [Bridge](/use-cases/bridge) — bring users in from other ecosystems
- [Orchestrate](/use-cases/orchestrate) — turn a multi-step entry into one action

## Case studies

- **LI.FI** powers millions of swaps across apps with Mayan routes.
- **OKX** integrated Mayan to power swaps across its product suite.
- **Bungee** unlocked Solana access with a single Mayan integration.
