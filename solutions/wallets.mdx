Mayan is the movement layer inside wallets. Wallets including MetaMask, Phantom, OKX Wallet and Backpack run their swap experience on Mayan: the interface is theirs, and the routing and execution underneath is Mayan.

Your users already move between chains. Today most of them leave your wallet to do it.

## Earn on every swap

You set a referrer address and a fee in basis points, and it is collected onchain automatically on every swap your users make. No invoicing and nothing to reconcile. See [Fees & Earning](/build/fees-earning).

## Non-custodial end to end

Mayan is non-custodial from signing to arrival, so adding cross-chain does not change your wallet's security model. Users keep control the whole way through.

## Use cases

- [Swap](/use-cases/swap) — trade any asset to any asset in-app
- [Bridge](/use-cases/bridge) — move holdings between chains
- [Deposit & Withdraw](/use-cases/deposit) — fund and exit other apps from the wallet

## Case studies

- **MetaMask** improved cross-chain quote latency for millions of users with Mayan.
- **Gem Wallet** added cross-chain without compromising its non-custodial design.
