Your users already want to move between chains. Today most of them leave your wallet to do it.

Mayan puts that movement inside your product, and lets you earn on every swap that runs through it.

## Set your fee and earn on every swap

You set a referrer address and a fee in basis points, and it is collected onchain automatically on every swap your users make. No invoicing and nothing to reconcile. See [Fees & Earning](/build/fees-earning).

## Cross-chain without changing what your wallet is

Mayan is non-custodial from signing to arrival, so adding cross-chain does not touch your wallet's security model. Users keep control the whole way through.

## See it in action

- **MetaMask** improved cross-chain quote latency for millions of users with Mayan.
- **Gem Wallet** added cross-chain without compromising its non-custodial design.

## What they use

- [Swap](/use-cases/swap), to trade any asset to any asset in-app
- [Bridge](/use-cases/bridge), to move holdings between chains
- [Deposit & Withdraw](/use-cases/deposit), to fund and exit other apps from the wallet
