Wallets integrate Mayan to give their users in-app swapping across chains. MetaMask, Phantom, OKX Wallet and Backpack all run their swap experience this way: the interface belongs to the wallet, and routing and execution run underneath it.

## What the integration covers

- Any-to-any swaps, same-chain and cross-chain, from one API.
- No token list to maintain. Any token with a contract address is routable.
- Native gas delivered on the destination chain, so users can transact when they arrive.

Most wallets integrate through the [SDK](/build/sdk). The [widget](/integration/swap-widget) is a drop-in alternative if you would rather not build the interface.

## Fees

You set a referrer address and a fee in basis points on the quote request. The fee is collected onchain on every swap routed through your integration, with no invoicing and nothing to reconcile. See [fees & earning](/build/fees-earning).

## Custody

The integration is non-custodial end to end. The user signs on the source chain, and funds move between their own address and the destination address they chose. No part of the flow takes custody, so a wallet's security model is unchanged.

## Use cases

- [Swap](/use-cases/swap) — trade any asset to any asset in-app
- [Bridge](/use-cases/bridge) — move holdings between chains
- [Deposit & Withdraw](/use-cases/deposit) — fund and exit other apps from the wallet

## Case studies

- **MetaMask** improved cross-chain quote latency for millions of users with Mayan.
- **Gem Wallet** added cross-chain without compromising its non-custodial design.
