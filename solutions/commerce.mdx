Payment and commerce products integrate Mayan so a buyer can pay in whatever asset they hold while the business settles in one asset. The conversion happens in transit, and neither side interacts with a swap.

## What the integration covers

- The buyer's token and chain are the inputs, your settlement asset is the output, and neither has to match. A customer pays 0.16 ETH on Ethereum, and you receive 250 USDC.
- **Exact-out settlement** on USDC, USDT and ETH routes, so the price you display is the amount that arrives. See [guaranteed price](/features/guaranteed-price).
- **Fixed deposit addresses** for repeat payers, who send funds like an ordinary transfer without connecting a wallet or signing. See [deposit addresses](/features/deposit-addresses).

## Why the amount has to be exact

An ordinary swap settles between an expected output and a minimum. A checkout cannot work that way: the number shown to the customer has to be the number that lands.

On supported routes Mayan settles exact-out, with drivers bidding on how much input they require rather than how much output they deliver.
