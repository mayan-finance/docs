Chains integrate Mayan so users can reach them from every other ecosystem Mayan supports, holding whatever they already hold.

## What launching with Mayan unlocks

- **Inbound from anywhere.** Users arrive from any supported chain with any supported token and land in the asset your ecosystem runs on.
- **Native gas on arrival.** [Gas on destination](/application/gas-on-destination) delivers your chain's native token alongside the transfer, so a new user can transact immediately.
- **Reachable on day one.** Coverage is not built chain by chain. Once a chain is live on Mayan, every supported asset and source chain is reachable at once.

## Use cases

- [Bridge](/use-cases/bridge): users arrive from anywhere
- [Deposit & Withdraw](/use-cases/deposit): apps on your chain are easy to fund and exit
- [Swap](/use-cases/swap): any-to-any trading once they land

## Case studies

- [**Solana**](https://mayan.finance/blog/mayan-the-way-to-solana) is the largest cross-chain destination in the network, with over \$6B landed through Mayan.
- **Monad** launched connected by Mayan on day one and has become one of the fastest-growing chains.
