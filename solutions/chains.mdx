Mayan is the movement layer connecting chains to each other. Solana, Monad, Base, HyperEVM, Sui and others are reachable through it, so users arrive from wherever they already hold assets.

A chain that users cannot easily reach is a chain they do not use.

## Reachable on day one

Users arrive from any supported chain with any supported token and land in the asset your ecosystem runs on. Nobody has to find a route in, or wait for one to exist.

## Both directions

Capital that arrives has to be able to leave, or it does not arrive in the first place. Mayan handles inbound and outbound movement, which is part of what makes an ecosystem worth entering.

## Use cases

- [Bridge](/use-cases/bridge) — users arrive from anywhere
- [Deposit & Withdraw](/use-cases/deposit) — apps on your chain are easy to fund and exit
- [Swap](/use-cases/swap) — any-to-any trading once they land

## Case studies

- **Solana.** Over \$6B has landed on Solana through Mayan, making it the largest cross-chain destination in the network.
- **Monad** launched connected by Mayan on day one and has become one of the fastest-growing chains.
