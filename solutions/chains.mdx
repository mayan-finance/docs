A new chain's hardest problem is not building it. It is being reachable.

With Mayan, any-to-any swaps are active from day one, so users arrive from every major ecosystem holding whatever they already have.

## Reachable from everywhere on day one

Users arrive from any supported chain with any supported token and land in the asset your ecosystem runs on. Nobody has to find a route in, or wait for one to exist.

## Built to be a destination

Capital that arrives has to be able to leave, or it does not arrive in the first place. Mayan handles both directions, which is part of what makes an ecosystem worth entering.

## See it in action

- **Solana**: over \$6B has landed on Solana through Mayan, making it the largest cross-chain destination in the network.
- **Monad** launched connected by Mayan on day one and has become one of the fastest-growing chains.

## What they use

- [Bridge](/use-cases/bridge), so users can arrive from anywhere
- [Deposit & Withdraw](/use-cases/deposit), so apps on your chain are easy to fund and exit
- [Swap](/use-cases/swap), for any-to-any trading once they land
