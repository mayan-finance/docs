Stablecoins are Mayan's most moved asset, with over **\$8B** moved cross-chain. Mayan supports every major stablecoin, and Fast MCTP gives those transfers a route built for ultra-low slippage.

## How it works

The person paying uses whatever they already hold, on whatever chain they hold it. The person receiving gets the asset they settle in. A buyer pays 0.16 ETH on Ethereum, 250 USDC lands in the merchant's account, and the buyer never sees a swap.

On USDC, USDT and ETH routes, [Guaranteed Price](/features/guaranteed-price) locks the output before the payment is confirmed, so the amount you display is the amount that lands. That is what makes a checkout possible.

Where the same payer sends repeatedly, they can pay to a fixed address instead of signing each time. See [Deposit Addresses](/features/deposit-addresses).

## Examples

- A fintech accepts many assets and settles in one.
- A payroll or remittance app pays recipients in the asset and on the chain they want.
- A merchant checkout displays a price and receives that exact amount.
- A wallet adding payments lets users send without picking a chain first.

## Case studies

- **PrimeVault** rebalances stablecoin reserves across ecosystems and pays a vendor on one chain from a treasury on another, without assets leaving the vault.
