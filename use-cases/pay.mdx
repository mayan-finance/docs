Stablecoins are Mayan's most moved asset, with over **\$8B** moved across chains to date.

A payment is not a trade. The number has to hold, and the money has to arrive now.

## What you get

- **The amount you quote is the amount that lands**, through [Guaranteed Price](/features/guaranteed-price) on USDC, USDT and ETH routes.
- **Settlement in seconds.**
- **Every major stablecoin**, with Fast MCTP built for ultra-low slippage.
- **The payer uses whatever they hold**, and the recipient gets the asset they settle in.
- **A permanent address for repeat payers.** See [Deposit Addresses](/features/deposit-addresses).

## How it works

Guaranteed Price locks the output before the payment is confirmed, so what you bill and what the recipient receives are the same number. That is what makes a checkout possible: you display a price and it holds. The payer never sees a swap, and where a flow needs it they can pay by sending to a fixed address rather than signing anything at all.

## How teams use it

- Fintechs and payment companies accept many assets and settle in one.
- Payroll and remittance apps pay recipients in the asset and on the chain they want.
- Merchant checkout displays a price and receives that exact amount.
- Treasury teams rebalance across chains from where their assets are already held.
- Wallets adding payments let users send without picking a chain first.
