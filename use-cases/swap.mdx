Swapping on Mayan means a user trades one asset for another without thinking about routes, bridges, liquidity sources, or which chain anything lives on. They pick what they hold and what they want, and it arrives instantly.

Every swap is competed for by Mayan's driver network, so users get the best execution through one simple flow.

To date Mayan has settled **\$20B\+** across **9M\+ swaps** for **3M\+ users**, and it is the same execution running behind the swap experience in some of the most-used wallets and apps in crypto.

## What your users get

- **Settlement in seconds.** Trades finish while the user is still on the screen, so they can act on the result straight away.
- **Any asset to any asset.** Long-tail tokens are tradable, not just the majors.
- **Any chain to any chain.** A user can trade out of what they hold on one chain and into what they want on another, in a single move.
- **The best price available.** Drivers compete for every order, so the price gets bid up rather than set.
- **Non-custodial.** Nobody takes custody at any point between signing and arrival.

## How teams use it

Wallets use Swap to add in-app trading. Aggregators and DEXs use it to widen asset coverage without adding integrations one by one. Trading apps use it to make every market reachable from a single interface.
