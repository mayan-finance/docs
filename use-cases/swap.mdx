Users pick what they hold and what they want, and it arrives instantly. No routes to choose, no bridges to find, no thinking about which chain anything lives on.

Mayan has settled **\$20B\+** across **9M\+ swaps** for **3M\+ users**, running behind the swap experience in some of the most-used wallets and apps in crypto.

## The market competes for every order

The user sets the terms up front: what they are sending, what they want back, and the minimum they will accept. That minimum is part of the instruction, so the swap only executes if it is met.

Drivers then bid to fill the order using their own capital, and the best bid wins.

<Info>
  **What this means:** every swap on Mayan is a live auction. The market competes to win your order, and that competition is what drives pricing in your favor.
</Info>

## Any token, not just the liquid ones

Any token with a contract address is tradable, with no whitelist and no listing process.

Long-tail assets trade the same way the majors do, and the same path covers a trade inside one chain and a trade across two.

## How teams use it

- Wallets add in-app trading without building routing.
- Aggregators and DEXs widen asset coverage without adding integrations one by one.
- Trading apps make every market reachable from one interface.
