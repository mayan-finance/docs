Users pick what they hold and what they want, and it arrives instantly. No routes to choose, no bridges to find, no thinking about which chain anything lives on.

Mayan has settled **\$20B\+** across **9M\+ swaps** for **3M\+ users**, running behind the swap experience in some of the most-used wallets and apps in crypto.

## What you get

- **Best price on every order.** Drivers compete in a live [auction](/architecture/auction), so the price is bid up rather than set.
- **Settlement in seconds.**
- **Any token with a contract address.** No whitelist, no listing process.
- **Same-chain and cross-chain** through the same path.
- **Non-custodial**, from signing to arrival.

## How it works

The user sets the terms up front: what they are sending, what they want back, and the minimum they will accept. That minimum is part of the instruction, so the swap only executes if it is met. Drivers then bid to fill the order using their own capital, and the best bid wins.

## How teams use it

- Wallets add in-app trading without building routing.
- Aggregators and DEXs widen asset coverage without adding integrations one by one.
- Trading apps make every market reachable from one interface.
