---
title: "Orchestrate"
description: "Attach any onchain action to a single swap."
---

Orchestrate is a swap with an instruction attached. The funds and a payload travel together, and the payload executes on the destination chain as the funds land.

Without it, entering a protocol from another chain is four user actions: move funds, swap, switch apps, deposit. With it, one.

## How it works

You encode a payload on the quote request. Mayan delivers the funds and the payload to your destination contract in the same transaction, and your contract acts on them.

- **Deposit.** Funds arrive and go straight into a protocol or vault.
- **Stake.** Assets are staked on arrival, so users start earning without a second step.
- **Mint, join or participate.** The transfer triggers it as the funds land.
- **Your own logic.** Encoded contract calls, IDs, session data, or routing information your app needs.

## Requirements

Orchestrate is the one part of Mayan that is not a drop-in. It needs two things on your side:

- A **destination contract** that can receive the funds and decode your payload.
- A **custom relayer** to deliver the order on the destination chain.

See [Zaps](/integration/custom-payload) for the payload format and relayer setup.

## Examples

- A vault turns a multi-step entry into one click.
- A yield app puts deposits to work the moment they land.
- A trading app funds a position and opens it in the same action.
