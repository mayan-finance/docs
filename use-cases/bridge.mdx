Moving an asset from one chain to another on Mayan is a single action. The user says where their assets are and where they need to be, and it arrives in seconds.

## Moving between chains stops feeling like a step

It used to be its own task: pick a route, wait minutes, land holding a wrapped asset, then go find gas before you could use it. On Mayan:

- **It finishes in seconds**, not minutes.
- **The user lands in the asset they asked for**, with no wrapped placeholder to deal with afterwards.
- **Gas is handled.** [Gas on destination](/application/gas-on-destination) puts native gas in the wallet on arrival, and [gasless execution](/features/gasless-execution) can take the gas step off the front on supported routes.
- **Size does not change the experience.** Transfers up to \$10M route through Mayan, with execution quality holding as the amount grows.
- **Most users never notice it happened.** A large share of the moves Mayan settles happen without the user knowing Mayan was the route.

## Secured by a decentralized messaging network

Every cross-chain move depends on one chain being told, reliably, what happened on another. That is the part worth understanding.

- **19 independent organizations attest every message.** Wormhole's Guardian network requires 13 of them to sign before a cross-chain message is valid.
- **The user is never the one carrying the risk.** The driver delivers on the destination chain first and can only unlock the funds on the source chain after that delivery has been verified, so the re-org risk sits with the driver.
- **No hidden custody.** At no point in a swap does Mayan take custody of funds.
- **Public contracts, public audits.** Audited by OtterSec and Sec3, with every transaction visible onchain and in [Mayan Explorer](/application/mayan-explorer).

<Info>
  **What this means:** the move runs through public, auditable contracts rather than infrastructure you cannot inspect. See [Security & Audits](/resources/audits).
</Info>

## How teams use it

- **Wallets** let users move holdings between chains without leaving the app.
- **Chains** become reachable from every major ecosystem on day one.
- **Apps and aggregators** meet users wherever they already hold assets.
