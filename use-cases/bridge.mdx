The user says where their assets are and where they need to be, and it arrives in seconds.

Getting between chains used to be its own task: pick a route, wait minutes, land holding a wrapped asset, then find gas before you could use it. None of that applies here.

## What you get

- **Settlement in seconds**, not minutes.
- **The asset they asked for**, with no wrapped placeholder to deal with.
- **Gas handled on arrival**, through [Gas on Destination](/application/gas-on-destination) and [Gasless Execution](/features/gasless-execution).
- **Size does not change the experience.** Transfers route up to \$10M.
- **Secured by Wormhole's decentralized messaging network.** See [Security & Audits](/resources/audits).

## How it works

Every cross-chain move depends on one chain being told, reliably, what happened on another. Wormhole's Guardian network attests each message, requiring 13 of 19 independent organizations to sign before it is valid. The driver delivers on the destination chain first and only unlocks the source funds once that delivery is verified, so the risk sits with them rather than the user. Mayan never takes custody at any point.

## How teams use it

- Wallets let users move holdings between chains without leaving the app.
- Chains become reachable from every major ecosystem on day one.
- Apps and aggregators meet users wherever they already hold assets.
