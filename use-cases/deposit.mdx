Getting money into an app and getting it back out are the two moments that decide whether someone uses your product.

On Mayan both are a single move. Users arrive with whatever they already hold, and leave holding whatever they actually want.

## What you get

- **One transaction in.** Any supported token on any supported chain, funds ready to use in seconds.
- **One transaction out.** The user picks the chain and the token, funds go straight to their wallet.
- **No manual conversion**, no switching between interfaces, no waiting in between.
- **A permanent address for repeat funding.** See [Deposit Addresses](/features/deposit-addresses).
- **Non-custodial** throughout.

## How it works

Funding used to mean working backwards from whatever an app accepted: move to the right chain, swap into the right asset, then deposit. Leaving meant the same in reverse with waiting added. Mayan collapses both into one move, so a trader can start with SOL on Solana, deposit into a venue, trade, and leave holding ETH on Ethereum. Any token any chain in, any token any chain out.

## How teams use it

- DEXs and trading venues let users fund from the asset they already hold.
- Exchanges and apps with internal balances drop the conversion step users do by hand.
- Onramps and checkout flows take whatever the user brings and settle in the asset the business runs on.
- Vaults and protocols accept deposits in assets they do not otherwise support.
- Any product that pays users out lets them receive in the asset and on the chain they want.
