Mayan is how crypto moves. Through a simple swap interface and a powerful set of developer tools, Mayan finds the best route across chains and liquidity and executes each swap behind the scenes, so people get fast, competitive swaps and teams can build cross-chain movement into their own products.

To date, Mayan has processed **\$19B\+ in volume** across **9M\+ swaps** for **3M\+ users**, moving assets between major chains including Solana, Ethereum, Monad, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Sui, and HyperEVM.

Mayan powers some of the biggest apps in crypto and fintech, including MetaMask, OKX Wallet, Phantom, MoonPay, SwapKit, LI.FI, and more.

## Instant money movement in any direction

Mayan turns cross-chain movement into a single step, so money can go any direction your users need: any token to any token, and any chain to any chain. Every order runs through an onchain auction where drivers compete to fill it, so pricing moves in the user's favor and settlement lands in as little as one second. All of it is reachable through one integration, giving your product instant cross-chain movement without the infrastructure to build or maintain yourself.

## Why teams build on Mayan

**Instant settlement.** Swaps complete in seconds end to end, because [drivers](/architecture/swift-v2) hold capital on the destination chain and fill from it the moment your user signs, so users get the fill they wanted in the moment they wanted it.

**Competitive pricing.** Drivers compete in real-time [auctions](/architecture/auction) on every order, giving users consistently competitive fills without your team managing pool depth or liquidity.

**Go direct.** Aggregators route to an execution provider, and Mayan is one of those providers, so integrating directly removes a hop and the fee layered on top of it. Your users get the price and the fill at the source, your team owns the execution relationship, and you [earn a fee](/build/fees-earning) on every swap you route.

**Size moves like everything else.** Three methods sit behind every quote, Swift, MCTP, and [Wormhole Swap](/architecture/wh-swap), and the auction routes automatically as transaction size changes, so a million-dollar move takes the same single signature as a hundred-dollar one.

**One click across chains.** Route selection, execution, and delivery on the destination chain all happen out of sight, so your users [move without leaving your product](/how-mayan-works/intent-based-swaps).

**Any token, any chain, one integration.** Swap, Bridge, Pay, Deposit, Withdraw, and Orchestrate all run through the same [SDK](/build/sdk), covering any token with a contract address and no preset whitelist across supported chains.

## How it works

Under the hood, every swap routes through one of three methods, Swift, MCTP, or Wormhole Swap, and the auction selects the one that best fits each order. You reach all of it through the Mayan SDK and API. Teams that want a ready-made interface can also drop in the Mayan widget, which is optional.
