Mayan is an intent-based cross-chain protocol built for speed, reliability and low-slippage execution.

Users move any asset to any chain in seconds, through one integration.

More than **\$20B** has moved through Mayan, across **9M\+ swaps** and **3M\+ users**, on Solana, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, BNB Smart Chain, Monad, Sui, HyperEVM and more.

## Three ways to use Mayan

- **Swap directly.** [swap.mayan.finance](https://swap.mayan.finance) is Mayan's own interface, open to anyone.
- **Build with it.** The [SDK](/build/sdk), API and [Widget](/integration/swap-widget) put cross-chain movement inside your own product.
- **Already running it.** Mayan powers the swap tabs of wallets like MetaMask, Phantom, OKX Wallet and Backpack, and the routes behind aggregators and trading apps including LI.FI, Jumper, Bungee and SwapKit. Most users never see Mayan, by design.
