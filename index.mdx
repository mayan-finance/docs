Mayan is how crypto moves. Through a simple swap interface and a powerful set of developer tools, Mayan finds the best route across chains and liquidity and executes each swap behind the scenes, so people get fast, competitive swaps and teams can build cross-chain movement into their own products.

To date, Mayan has processed **\$19B\+ in volume** across **9M\+ swaps** for **3M\+ users**, moving assets between major chains including Solana, Ethereum, Monad, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Sui, and HyperEVM.

Mayan powers some of the biggest apps in crypto and fintech, including MetaMask, OKX Wallet, Phantom, MoonPay, SwapKit, LI.FI, and more.

## Instant money movement in any direction

Mayan turns cross-chain movement into a single step, so money can go any direction your users need: any token to any token, and any chain to any chain. Every order runs through an onchain auction where drivers compete to fill it, so pricing moves in the user's favor and settlement lands in as little as one second. All of it is reachable through one integration, giving your product instant cross-chain movement without the infrastructure to build or maintain yourself.

## Why teams build on Mayan

**Instant settlement.** Cross-chain swaps that feel like same-chain swaps, landing in seconds rather than minutes or hours, because [drivers](/architecture/swift-v2) already hold capital on the destination chain and fill from it the moment your user signs.

**Every price is competed for.** Every order goes to a real-time [onchain auction](/architecture/auction) where drivers bid against each other to fill it, so execution stays competitive without you configuring liquidity or maintaining a fixed route.

**Go direct.** Aggregators route to an execution provider, and Mayan is one of those providers, so coming to us directly removes a hop and the fee layered on top of it. Your users get the price and the fill at the source, and your team owns the execution relationship and [earns a partner fee](/build/fees-earning) on the swaps it routes.

**Size moves like everything else.** Mayan is built for low-slippage execution, and three methods sit behind every quote, Swift, MCTP, and [Wormhole Swap](/architecture/wh-swap), with the auction picking the one that fits the order. A million-dollar move takes the same single signature as a hundred-dollar one.

**One click for your users.** Route selection, execution, and delivery on the destination chain all happen out of sight, which turns a multi-step onchain process into [one action](/how-mayan-works/intent-based-swaps) inside your product.

**One integration, any token to any chain.** Swap, Bridge, Pay, Deposit, Withdraw, and Orchestrate all run through the same [SDK](/build/sdk), with broad token coverage across supported chains and flexible partner fees, so you build once and get the whole surface.

## How it works

Under the hood, every swap routes through one of three methods, Swift, MCTP, or Wormhole Swap, and the auction selects the one that best fits each order. You reach all of it through the Mayan SDK and API. Teams that want a ready-made interface can also drop in the Mayan widget, which is optional.
