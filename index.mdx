Mayan is how crypto moves. Through a simple swap interface and a powerful set of developer tools, Mayan finds the best route across chains and liquidity and executes each swap behind the scenes, so people get fast, competitive swaps and teams can build cross-chain movement into their own products.

To date, Mayan has processed **\$19B\+ in volume** across **9M\+ swaps** for **3M\+ users**, moving assets between major chains including Solana, Ethereum, Monad, BNB Chain, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Sui, and HyperEVM.

Mayan powers some of the biggest apps in crypto and fintech, including MetaMask, OKX Wallet, Phantom, MoonPay, SwapKit, LI.FI, and more.

## Instant money movement in any direction

Mayan turns cross-chain movement into a single step, so money can go any direction your users need: any token to any token, and any chain to any chain.

All of it is reachable through one integration, giving your product instant cross-chain movement without the infrastructure to build or maintain yourself.

<Info>
  **What this means:** you integrate once, and your users move any token across any chain, in seconds, without leaving your product.
</Info>

</CardGroup>

## How it works

Under the hood, every swap routes through one of three methods, Swift, MCTP, or Wormhole Swap, and the auction selects the one that best fits each order. You reach all of it through the Mayan SDK and API. Teams that want a ready-made interface can also drop in the Mayan widget, which is optional.
