---
title: "Gasless Execution"
description: "Let builders remove gas friction for their users on supported flows."
---

Gasless execution lets a user start a move without holding the source chain's native token. Rather than sending a transaction, they sign a message, and a Mayan relayer submits the transaction on their behalf.

It is scoped to specific flows rather than available on every route.

## Requesting it

Pass `gasless: true` on the quote request. Eligible quotes come back with `gasless` set in the response, and you surface that to the user before they sign. See the [Quote API](/integration/quote-api).
