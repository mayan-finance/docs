---
title: "Why Mayan"
description: "What Mayan gives you that a fixed route does not."
---

Any asset to any chain instantly, through one integration.

- **Instant settlement.** Swaps complete in seconds, not minutes. Whether someone is swapping on swap.mayan.finance or inside a wallet running on Mayan, the money moves when they ask for it.
- **Competitive pricing.** Prices are competed for rather than quoted. The market bids on every order in a [real-time auction](/architecture/auction), so the price is the best the market can produce at that moment.
- **One-click simplicity.** A cross-chain swap feels the same as a same-chain swap. One signature, and everything else happens underneath.
- **Any token, any chain.** Any token with a contract address, across every chain Mayan supports. Nothing to whitelist, nothing to add token by token.
- **Built for size.** Three swap methods sit behind Mayan, [Swift](/architecture/swift-v2), [MCTP](/architecture/mctp) and [Wormhole Swap](/architecture/wh-swap), so moving $$1M is as straightforward as moving $$100. High-value transfers run up to \$10M in a single transaction.
- **Go direct.** Mayan is the execution layer itself, not a router sitting on top of one. Fewer hops, fewer middlemen, and the [fee](/build/fees-earning) on every swap is yours.
- **One integration.** Wherever a user needs to go, whatever token they need to hold, and whatever they need to do onchain once they arrive, it runs through the same [SDK and API](/build/sdk).
- **Non-custodial.** Nobody takes custody of a user's assets at any point.
