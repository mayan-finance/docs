Swapping on [swap.mayan.finance](https://swap.mayan.finance) takes three steps, whether the tokens are on one chain or two.

1. Choose the **token you are sending** and the chain it is on.
2. Choose the **chain you want to receive on** and the **token you want**.
3. Confirm and sign once. Mayan routes the swap and delivers the output to your wallet.

Any major wallet works.

## Reading the quote

Every quote shows two numbers: the amount you can expect to receive, and the minimum you will accept. The swap will not execute below that minimum.

On USDC, USDT and ETH routes the amount is locked before you confirm, so the number you are shown is exactly the number you receive. See [Guaranteed Price](/features/guaranteed-price).

Check the price impact percentage before confirming. It shows how much your swap moves the price on that market, and a high number means you are not getting a good rate.

## Example: Solana to Base

- Connect your wallet
- Select Solana as the source chain and your input token
- Select Base as the destination chain and your output token
- Confirm and execute

Your tokens arrive on Base once the swap completes.

Every swap is visible on [Mayan Explorer](/application/mayan-explorer), searchable by transaction hash or wallet address.
